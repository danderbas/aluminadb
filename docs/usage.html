aluminadb — usage

Three real questions, answered with a single query each against real 2019-2020 production data — no synthetic examples. Every query is shown before its result.

0. Monthly report

Day-by-day yield for a month, a manual sum verifying it against the dedicated monthly view, then every month on record in one query.

1. Shift planning

Most urgent open orders, what's already covered by stock, whether the dies behind today's plan have room before their next nitriding, and what fits in a 50-billet first shift.

2. Nitriding due list

Every die ranked by how close it is to needing nitriding again, most urgent first.
